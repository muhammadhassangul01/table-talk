Switching questions

How is TableTalk different from Slang.ai?

TableTalk is built to be simple and flat: $50/month for unlimited calls with no contracts and no enterprise sales process. It books reservations into OpenTable and Resy, texts menus, and goes live in minutes — not weeks.

Does TableTalk integrate with OpenTable and Resy like Slang.ai?

Yes. TableTalk books reservations directly into OpenTable and Resy in real time, and also works with Toast, SevenRooms, and Yelp.

Can I switch from Slang.ai to TableTalk easily?

Yes. Setup uses conditional call forwarding on your existing number, takes under 5 minutes, and there's no new hardware or long-term contract.

What happens to my existing reservations during a switch?

Nothing changes. TableTalk writes new bookings straight into your existing OpenTable or Resy account, so your reservation history and staff workflows stay exactly the same.
