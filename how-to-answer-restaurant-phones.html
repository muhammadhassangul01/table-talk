How to answer restaurant phones during rush hour — without losing a single reservation

The phone starts ringing at 6 PM, and your host is already seating a line out the door. Here's why that happens, what it costs, and how to fix it for good.

6 min read Updated August 2026

Why restaurants miss 1 in 5 calls at peak

The math is brutal. Between 6 and 8 PM your host is seating tables, your manager is on the floor, and the phone is the furthest thing from anyone's hands. The average restaurant loses 1 in 5 calls during peak hours — and each one of those callers is a party booking at the restaurant down the street.

The average check for a party of four is roughly $120. Lose five calls a night, and that's $600 in revenue walking away — every single night. It's not a staffing problem, it's a structural one: a phone line needs someone to answer it at the exact moment everyone is busiest.

Five ways to stop missing reservation calls

1 Give the host stand a dedicated line

A separate reservation line means calls ring somewhere specific instead of every phone in the building. It helps — but it doesn't solve the core problem: at 7 PM on Saturday, that dedicated phone still rings into an empty host stand.

2 Cross-train a phone person for the rush

Assign one person to answer calls during the 6–8 PM window. It works until they're pulled to seat a table — which is exactly what happens on every busy night. It's also the most expensive option: a dedicated phone host runs $30–50/hour.

3 Upgrade your voicemail message

A good voicemail that directs callers to your online booking link recovers a few. But guests who wanted to talk to a human rarely leave a message — they hang up and call the next place. Voicemail is a safety net, not a solution.

4 Push everyone to online booking

Your OpenTable and Resy widgets capture guests already on your website. But millions of reservations still start with a phone call — especially from older guests, large parties, and special occasions. Online booking never replaces the phone; it only supplements it.
