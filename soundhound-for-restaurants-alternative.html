Switching questions

How is TableTalk different from SoundHound for Restaurants?

SoundHound sells a broad voice platform used in drive-thrus, cars, and TV remotes — restaurants are one product line. TableTalk is a restaurant phone specialist: flat $50/month, unlimited calls, native OpenTable and Resy booking, and a 5-minute setup using your existing number.

Does TableTalk integrate with OpenTable and Resy?

Yes. New reservations are booked live into OpenTable and Resy, and TableTalk works with Toast, SevenRooms, and Yelp too. Your existing reservation history is untouched.

Can I switch from SoundHound to TableTalk easily?

Yes. TableTalk runs on conditional call forwarding, keeps your existing number, and takes under 5 minutes to go live. No new hardware, no new number, no long-term contract.

What if a caller needs a real person?

The AI recognizes when a call needs a human and transfers to your host stand or manager with a quick summary of the call — so nobody repeats themselves.
