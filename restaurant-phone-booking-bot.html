A restaurant phone booking bot is an AI that answers your incoming calls and books reservations over the phone — checking live availability, confirming the party size and time, and writing the booking straight into OpenTable or Resy.

No. TableTalk's voices sound warm and human with natural pauses, and you can pick a voice and greeting that match your brand. Most callers assume they're talking to a person.

What if the caller needs a human?

The bot recognizes those moments and transfers the call to your host stand or manager — with a summary of the conversation so no one repeats themselves.
