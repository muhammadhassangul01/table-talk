Toast questions, answered

Can TableTalk take phone orders for a Toast POS restaurant?

Yes. The AI takes the order, confirms pickup time, collects the guest's contact info for SMS confirmation, and hands the complete order to your Toast flow so your staff review it before pressing send.

Does TableTalk replace my Toast online ordering?

No — it feeds the same kitchen. TableTalk handles the phone calls your online ordering page can't, so dining room and phone order traffic both reach your Toast system the same way.

How does TableTalk know my Toast menu prices?

We sync from your live Toast menu catalog, so items, modifiers, prices, and availability stay current without manual menu upkeep.

Do we also need a reservations system for this to work?

No. TableTalk works with just Toast. If you also take reservations, the same line can connect to OpenTable or Resy too.
