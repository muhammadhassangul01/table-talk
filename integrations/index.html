Integration FAQ

Will my OpenTable/Resy data stay clean?

Yes. TableTalk writes bookings the same way your host would — correct guest count, time, and contact info — and confirms every booking by SMS so no-shows and misreads drop.

Does TableTalk work during a Toast POS outage?

The phone AI keeps answering and taking guest details even if an integration is temporarily down, then flags anything it couldn't sync for a quick human check.

That's exactly what the 7-day free trial covers. Route (or temporarily forward) your line, make a test call, and watch the booking land in your OpenTable, Resy, or Toast setup.
