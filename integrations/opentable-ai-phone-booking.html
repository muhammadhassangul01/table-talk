OpenTable questions, answered

How does OpenTable phone booking work with TableTalk?

A caller asks for a table, the AI checks availability against your OpenTable calendar, books the reservation live under the caller's name and party size, and confirms by SMS.

Is it really a native OpenTable integration?

Yes. Reservations are written directly into your OpenTable account in real time, so your book, floor plan, and staff workflows stay exactly the same.

What happens if OpenTable is slow or down?

The AI still takes the caller's details and flags the booking for a quick human entry if the connection hiccups. No call is ever dropped.

Does it work with Resy and Toast too?

Yes. If you run multiple systems, one TableTalk line can handle OpenTable, Resy, and Toast POS without changing how you work.
